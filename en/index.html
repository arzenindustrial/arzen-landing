About Arzen

The verification layer your procurement team doesn't have time to build.

A procurement director can find a hundred CNC shops in a Google search. What that search can't tell you is whether any of them can actually hold your tolerance, hit your date, and answer the phone when something goes wrong.

We close that gap by doing the unglamorous work before you ever see a name: walking the shop floor, checking the equipment list against real parts, sitting across the table from the owner. That verification work is the entire service. Sourcing, quoting and order management follow from it.

2

  • Human relationship. We've already done the hard verification work so you don't have to.

Arzen is a sourcing and verification intermediary for U.S. procurement teams. We don't manufacture and we don't hold inventory. We connect you directly with pre-verified CNC, tooling and structural suppliers in Querétaro and Nuevo León, and stay involved through your first purchase order.
