About Arzen

The trust layer between U.S. buyers and Mexican precision manufacturers.

Arzen exists because of a trust gap, not a technology gap. A procurement director in the U.S. can find a hundred CNC shops in a search — what they can't find is certainty that any of them can actually hold the tolerance, hit the date, and answer the phone when something goes wrong.

We close that gap by doing the unglamorous work first: walking the shop floor, checking the equipment list against the part, and building a relationship with the owner before we ever put a supplier in front of a buyer. That verification work is the entire business — everything else follows from it.
