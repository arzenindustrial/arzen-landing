Frequently asked questions

What buyers ask before their first order.

What does Arzen actually verify before recommending a supplier?

We visit the shop floor in person, confirm the equipment list against real parts in production, check documented tolerances and capacity, and cross-reference the shop's track record with its existing clients. That verification is the entire service — sourcing and quoting follow from it.

Is sourcing from Querétaro or Nuevo León USMCA-qualifying?

Yes. Suppliers in our network are located in Querétaro and Nuevo León, Mexico, and sourcing through them avoids the tariff exposure associated with parts sourced from China, while cutting freight time from weeks by sea to hours by truck across the border.

What industries and part types does Arzen focus on?

We stay deliberately narrow: tooling, fixtures and secondary structural components for aerospace OEMs, Tier 1/2 suppliers, defense manufacturing programs, aviation MRO and space/satellite systems — not flight-critical primes requiring a 12–24 month certification cycle.

How much does it cost to work with Arzen?

Consulting engagements start at a fixed fee, discussed on your initial call once we understand your part, tolerances and volume. There is no cost to submit an inquiry.

How is Arzen different from a sourcing marketplace or broker?

Marketplaces rely on self-reported capabilities with no one having seen the shop floor. Independent brokers offer personal relationships but no documented, repeatable verification process. Arzen combines in-person verification, a documented process, and one accountable point of contact through your first purchase order.

What happens after I request a consultation?

Our team follows up within 1 business day to schedule a short call about your part, tolerances, certifications and volume. From there we present pre-verified shops matched to your spec, manage formal quoting and a physical sample part, and stay involved through your first production order.
